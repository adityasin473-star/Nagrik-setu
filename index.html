Built for how it already works

The same counter you know, just without the queue.

Every citizen in India already understands the token counter — take a number, wait your turn, get it stamped. Nagrik Setu keeps that familiar system, but puts it on your phone so you can track the stamp without standing in line.

Process

How a grievance moves through the office

01

Take a token

Register and file your complaint with details, category and location.

02

Get stamped Pending

Your ticket is logged instantly with a unique token ID for tracking.

03

Officer reviews it

The concerned desk moves it to In Progress once action begins.

04

Ticket is Resolved

You're notified on your dashboard once the issue is closed out.
